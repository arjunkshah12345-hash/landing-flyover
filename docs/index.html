Three scripts. Your machine.

No video model and no upload. A real browser, a WebGL2 page and ffmpeg, checked before the final render.

Capture

Lock the page

capture.mjs opens your page at 1440px and 2× scale, lets everything finish loading, collapses scroll-pinned sections and saves each lifting piece on transparency.

Film

Fly the camera

film.js lays the page on a studio floor and computes every frame from time alone: one speed curve, per-pixel depth of field, a shadow under every card.

Render

Print the MP4

render.mjs shoots 60fps with motion blur. flow.py proves the glide never stalls; score.py lands your drop on the title.

Built like a camera. Not a slideshow.

Every number is written into the skill, so the film comes out the same way on every page.
