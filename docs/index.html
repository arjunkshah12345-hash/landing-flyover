Call sheet

Four things. Asked once.

LocationYour landing pageAny public URL. Long pages simply glide faster.
TalentYour brand and logo markFour logo tiles lift off in the first second and come back for the end card.
DialogueA two-part closing lineA short statement, then an italic tail.
ScoreA track, if you have oneOptional. The drop is found and lands on 9.97 seconds, as the title appears.

Make a landing flyover of your.site. Close on “Ship it today,” then “Sleep tonight.”Everything you type. Seven stills come back for approval before the render.

Production

Three scripts. Your machine.

01Pre-production

Capture

capture.mjs opens your page at 1440px and 2× scale, scrolls it once so everything finishes loading, collapses scroll-pinned sections and saves the page plus every lifting piece on transparency.

02Principal photography

Film

film.js lays the page on a studio floor and computes every frame from time alone: one speed curve, per-pixel depth of field, a shadow under every card.

03Post

Render

render.mjs shoots 60fps with 16 motion-blur subframes. flow.py proves the glide never stalls; score.py lands the drop on the title.

Camera report

Built like a camera. Not a slideshow.

1880pxFocal length
56°Tilt off straight down
60fpsAt 1440 × 1152, H.264
16Motion-blur subframes per frame
40Depth-of-field taps per pixel
0Cuts

Never in the film: crossfades, stops or surges in the glide, the camera turning, zooming in during the flight, glows, or text over your page before the end card. An optical-flow pass on a preview checks the motion before the final render.
